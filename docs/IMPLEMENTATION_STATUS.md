## Acceptance scenarios

1. Existing Lithium: real autocomplete, full text + vectors, article/evidence, graph and timeline/compare navigation verified.
2. New scientific topic: real sources → cited extractive draft → human review verified. Full synthesis/triple/media workflow remains incomplete; no automated approval was used.
3. Fictional concept: seeded Vibranium stays explicitly fictional. Autonomous canon classification and research validation remain incomplete.
4. Discovery path: actual bounded verified connection and honest disconnected result verified. Unknown entities do not produce invented paths.
5. Evidence inspection: statement → claim → actual excerpt/source and explicit empty contradictory evidence verified.
6. Knowledge evolution: Lithium events, date precision, filters and source links verified.
7. Comparison: documented values and missing-data behavior verified. Dynamic inference of comparison dimensions is not implemented.
8. Broken source: safe check, affected-article quality issue and preserved history implemented; broader content-change monitoring pending.
9. AI failure: failed service preserves checkpoint and cannot publish; tested. No mandatory external LLM exists in the baseline.
10. Rollback: restoration creates a new revision and outbox event; immutable history and canonical hashes tested. Multi-index recovery under concurrent writers needs broader stress testing.
